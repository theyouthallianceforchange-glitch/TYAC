Sheet Y-02 Member Voices

Stories From Our Members

What young people have done with us so far — the campaigns they carried, and what it took on the day.

Voice 01 Quality Education — Peace Island

Carrying the school materials in

Members prepared and delivered learning materials into local schools and led the “Importance of Education” session at the Christian Academy. The visible part was an hour in a classroom; the real work was the sourcing, the packing and the follow-up with the school.

Voice 02 Environmental Sanitation — New Matadi

A full day on the estate corridors

Youth advocates took on the public corridors and residential blocks of the New Matadi Estate. Early start, gloves on, waste bagged and moved out — a sanitation campaign run entirely by young volunteers from the community itself.

Voice 03 Policy Advocacy — Montserrado

Taking community concerns into the room

Our members sit in on youth policy discussions and carry what they hear in their own communities into them — local governance first, national youth policy next. No experience needed to start; we prepare you before you speak.

Voice 04 Peacebuilding & Well-being — Communities

Talking first, then solving

Where a community is tense, volunteers open the conversation before anything else — listening, finding the root of the dispute instead of reacting to the loudest voice, and making room to talk about stress and mental well-being without stigma.

Sheet Y-03 Open Roles

What Volunteers Actually Do

Every role is field-based and hands-on, worked alongside a team lead. Pick the one you want — or take any role and we will place you.

R01

School Support Volunteer

Help get learning materials into local schools and run sessions with students — sourcing, packing, and standing in front of a classroom.

R02

Clean-up Volunteer

Join sanitation exercises across estates, corridors and water points: early starts, gloves on, waste bagged and moved out with the team.

R03

Community Mobiliser

Bring people out. You explain the campaign, move through the community with the team, and help organise the route and the day itself.

R04

Peer Dialogue Volunteer

Sit in on community conversations around peace, conflict and mental well-being. You listen first, never lecture, and you are briefed before every session.
